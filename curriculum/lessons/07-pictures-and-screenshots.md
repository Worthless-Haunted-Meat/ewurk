# Pictures and screenshots

EWURK topic: **Lesson 7 — Pictures and screenshots**

Instructor: read each numbered step aloud. Attendance is marked in EWURK after class—not on this page.

## Goal

Everyone can open an image from their `Saturday` folder, take a screenshot, and file it in the right folder without leaving pictures scattered on the desktop.

## Materials

- EWURK lab Linux desktop
- Files app and image viewer
- One **stock** image per machine copied ahead of time by staff to `Saturday/shared/sample.jpg` (use a royalty-free photo of a landscape or animal—no photos of real students)

## Steps

1. Open **Files** → `Saturday` → `shared` and double-click `sample.jpg` to view it. Zoom in and out if the viewer allows.
2. Create `Saturday/lesson-7/screenshots` if it does not exist. Explain screenshots are pictures of the screen—only take them when staff says it is OK.
3. Take a **screenshot** using the lab shortcut (often PrtScn or Shift+PrtScn; show the program’s posted cheat sheet). Open the screenshot from Pictures or the notification preview.
4. Move or save the screenshot into `Saturday/lesson-7/screenshots` with a clear name like `desktop-practice.png`. Delete any duplicate on the Desktop.
5. Close the image viewer. In the browser, open one approved image-heavy page (e.g. Wikipedia article with one photo) and discuss: “Would I share this screenshot publicly? Why or why not?”

## If the room is chaotic (10 minutes)

Only practice opening `sample.jpg` and creating the `screenshots` folder—skip screenshot shortcut if keys fight each other; staff demo once on the projector.

## Done looks like

- `Saturday/lesson-7/screenshots` exists with at least one image file per checked machine.
- Desktop has no leftover screenshot clutter.
- Learners can state one reason to be careful sharing screenshots (may show private messages or homework).
