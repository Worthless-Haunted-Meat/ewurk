# Email basics

EWURK topic: **Lesson 8 — Email basics**

Instructor: read each numbered step aloud. Attendance is marked in EWURK after class—not on this page.

## Goal

Everyone can sign in to a **practice** webmail inbox (provided by staff), read a message, and write a short reply—using fictional names only.

## Materials

- EWURK lab Linux desktop with browser
- Staff-created practice accounts on your organization’s webmail **or** a local demo inbox (no personal family email required)
- Printed card with fictional login: `learner.demo@example-class.test` (password given verbally in class, not written on handouts)

## Steps

1. Open the browser to the **webmail login page** your program approved. Explain email is like digital mail—only open messages from people you know or staff sent for class.
2. Sign in with the practice account on the card. Point out **Inbox**, **Compose**, and **Sign out** before anyone types.
3. Open one **pre-sent** message from staff (subject: “Saturday check-in”). Read it together. Do not click links in real unknown mail today—this message has no links.
4. Click **Reply** and type two sentences: a greeting and one thing they enjoyed in class this month. Use fictional names (`Friend`, `Instructor`) only—no home addresses or phone numbers.
5. **Send** the reply to the staff sender address shown. Sign out of webmail and close the browser tab.

## If the room is chaotic (10 minutes)

Staff demos reply on the projector while learners watch. Pairs describe aloud what they would write—no keyboards until the room settles.

## Done looks like

- Each pair or learner sent one short reply from a practice account (or staff confirms demo participation).
- Everyone signed out of webmail.
- No personal email passwords or real family inboxes used in class.
